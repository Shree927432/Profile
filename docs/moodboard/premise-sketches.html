Premise sketches

Rough illustrations to compare the feel of each room. Not the final look: that comes from Blender in Phase 4. Labels mark the clickable objects.

A. The practice room, 5:40 a.m.

Before dawn into first light. Quiet, focused, warm. Fan, metronome, crows, the piano.

B. The green room, 11:40 p.m. Saturday

Late night, warm and humid. Tired, buzzing, private. Muffled crowd through the wall, a chime when the run finishes.

C. The studio control room, 2 a.m.

Deep night, no windows, lit by equipment. Precise, nocturnal, absorbed. Speaker hiss, fader clicks, music when a fader goes up.
