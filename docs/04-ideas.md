# Ideas to go past the reference

Rule: pick one big bet and two small surprises for version 1. Everything else
waits. One fully working crazy idea beats twelve half-working ones.

## Make the place bigger than a desk
- A whole apartment or shop you walk through. Doors load the next room.
- A street at night. Each shop window is a project.
- A train car. The view changes by section. Posts are newspapers on seats.

## Respond to the real world
- Real local time and weather drive the window, the lights, the rain.
- Latest commit scrolls on a monitor. Current song on the stereo.
- The wall clock is right. The calendar shows today. The TV plays the last talk.

## Remember and be shared
- A guestbook that is a real pinboard. Notes persist. Others see them.
- Faint presence dots for other visitors in the room right now.
- The room notices a second visit. The coffee is gone. The light changed.

## A light game layer
- Five hidden objects. Find all, a drawer opens with something real.
- A working handheld with one tiny game. High scores persist.
- A puzzle on the whiteboard that leads to the email address.

## A mind in the room
- A phone on the desk. Pick it up and talk to an agent that knows the work.
- A typewriter that writes a note about the project you clicked.
- A radio DJ model that announces songs and reads post titles.

## Physical
- Generate the room from a 3D scan of the real desk.
- A real printed prop with a QR code that opens the site at that project.

## Break the medium
- The whole site is a tape. The scrollbar is tape. Fast forward through static.
- Favicon and tab title join the scene. Closing the tab turns the lamp off first.
- A print stylesheet that prints a brochure of the room.

## Our current pick (proposed 2026-10-19, owner to confirm)
Big bet: the instruments really play. Piano keys and guitar strings make real notes.
Small surprise 1: the window follows the real time and weather in Pune.
Small surprise 2: the plant grows a leaf each time you come back.
