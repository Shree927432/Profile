# About

I'm a builder exploring the frontier of AI.

My work sits at the intersection of AI engineering, agentic systems, LLMs, reasoning, and autonomous software. I'm interested in understanding how intelligent systems can move beyond generating outputs to reason, use tools, learn from feedback, and solve increasingly complex problems.

I learn by building: taking ideas apart from first principles, turning them into working systems, measuring what works, and iterating.

Right now, I'm focused on building AI agents and developer tools, experimenting with evaluations and reasoning systems, and developing the engineering foundations needed to work on increasingly capable AI.

Long term, I want to work on frontier technology, building systems and companies that push the boundary of what AI can do.
