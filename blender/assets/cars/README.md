# Car models (downloaded by the owner, 2026-11-02)

Four scale models on a slim white display shelf on the wall (owner: "Scale models on a shelf", cars: "f1, Porsche 992
GT3 R, Mercedes-AMG GT3 Evo, Mercedes-AMG ONE"). Real logos and sponsor liveries are repainted in our own colours (the
room carries no real logos, decision 2026-10-26). The full originals stay here untouched for the Blender file; only the
web version is cut down. The shelf loads as its own file after the room appears (owner: "Yes, load it after").
Files are git-ignored (too big); download them again from the links.
